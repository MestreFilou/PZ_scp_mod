# Texture credits

The tiles of the SCP site (tileset `scp_facility_02`, in
`Contents/mods/SCPFoundation/42/media/texturepacks/scp_facility.pack`) are made from textures of
**SCP - Containment Breach** (Joonas Rikkonen "Regalis" and contributors, source:
https://github.com/Regalis11/scpcb, folder `GFX/map`), licensed under
**Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

Source files used (kept in `art/cb_textures/`): `whitewall`, `officewall`, `concretewall`, `concretemoss`,
`metalpanels`, `concretefloor`, `tilefloor`, `dirtymetal`, `metal3`, `metal`, `Door01`, `containment_doors`,
`rockmoss`, `pdwall`, `misc`, `vent`, `controlpanel`.

Changes made for this mod: resized, cropped, recoloured and projected onto isometric tiles by
`tools/make_facility_tiles.py`; stripes, signs and furniture are drawn by that script.
Because of the ShareAlike clause, these tiles (and any edited version of them) stay under CC BY-SA 3.0.

The plan of the site is original; its proportions (hallways about 2 m wide, three zones in a row, Gate A reached
by a lift) follow the rooms of SCP - Containment Breach.

# Textures from SCP – Containment Breach

These files come from the source repository of **SCP – Containment Breach**
(https://github.com/Regalis11/scpcb, folder `GFX/map`), by Joonas "Regalis"
Rikkonen and contributors, based on the works of the SCP Foundation community.

They are licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0**
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Most were resized to 512 px.

`tools/make_facility_tiles.py` maps them onto Project Zomboid isometric tiles
(tileset `scp_facility_02`). The resulting tiles are a derivative work and are
released under the same licence.

The layout of the site (`tools/make_site_layout.py`) is original, but takes its
proportions from the game's room meshes: hallways about 2 m wide in 8 m cells,
three zones in a row (Light Containment, Heavy Containment, Entrance Zone)
separated by checkpoints, Gate A reached by a lift.
